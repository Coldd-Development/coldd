How this works

Which products. Every Monday at 00:00 UTC each eligible product gets a demand score blended from real signals: BuiltByBit sales of the same listing and of similar listings (recent sales count more), product page views on this site over the last 14 days, and paid units on this site over the last 30 days. The weights re-balance over whichever signals have data, so site sales take over as they build up. The runaway best seller is held back (it sells without help), anything discounted in the last two weeks is rotated out, and no more than two products come from one category. Products marked "Disallow from all sales" or excluded below are skipped.

How deep. Each deal is logged. The next run records the views and sales it earned, and the algorithm learns which discount depth earns the most revenue per visitor: it samples each depth's conversion rate from your own results and picks the depth with the best expected revenue (price after discount x conversion). With no results yet it tries the depths evenly, and it settles on the winner as data comes in. Depths come from the Max discount and Discount step above and never go past a product's minimum sale price or its own max discount.

It writes the discount using the same "Was price" field the product editor already uses for a manual sale, so it never conflicts with a sale you set by hand. It only ever touches products it discounted itself, and always restores the exact original price on revert or at the next run.

Robux checkout pool

One shared pool of gamepasses is leased out per order (priced to that order's exact total) instead of every product owning its own pass. This grows automatically when a checkout finds the pool empty - seeding it ahead of time just avoids the first buyer of the day paying that extra latency. Passes named coldd order pass … are managed here. To use one in the game yourself, just rename it off that auto-name - the next checkout that would draw it drops it from the pool automatically. Retire does the same on demand (and takes it off sale on Roblox).
